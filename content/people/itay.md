Itay is a Postdoctoral Researcher at the Open University of Israel. He completed his BSc, MSc, and PhD at the Sagol School of Neuroscience at Tel Aviv University, where he worked with [Liad Mudrik](https://mudriklab.sites.tau.ac.il/) on consciousness and unconscious processing. He is interested in understanding the mind and is curious about the potential of computational methods to advance that goal.
